A game project / 2018–2019

Dungeon
Explorer

A small dungeon shooter
I made in Processing.

You’re a wizard with two magic staffs and four rooms to explore. The demons, ogres, and zombies would rather you stayed home.

It’s a prototype: there’s combat and room exploration, but no campaign or save system.

Download the source ↓

Runs on your desktop with Processing.

A few recordings from development ↗

Try it out

  1. Install Processing and the ControlP5, Box2D, and Minim libraries.
  2. Download the source into a folder named DungeonExplorer.
  3. Open DungeonExplorer.pde and press Run.
